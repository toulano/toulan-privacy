Delete your Toulan Study account

Toulan Study · family education tracker · Privacy policy

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Toulan Study and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

It's deleted from our servers straight away, and the app on that phone is cleared.

By email

If you can't open the app, email us from the address you sign in with, so we can confirm it's your account:

Request deletion

We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your sign-in email, your place in the family and your reminder settings.
  • If you're the family's only parent: the whole family: children, teachers, classes, sessions and attendance, payments, expenses, homework, exams and every member's access.
  • Otherwise the family's data stays for its other members.

Deletion is permanent. To keep a copy, use More → Export data first. Data on a phone that's offline is removed from that phone the next time it opens Toulan Study and finds the account gone.
