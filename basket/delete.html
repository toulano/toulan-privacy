Delete your Toulan Basket account

Toulan Basket · household shopping memory · Privacy policy

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Toulan Basket.
  2. Go to Home → Delete account.
  3. Confirm.

It's deleted from our servers straight away, and the household's data is removed from that phone.

By email

If you can't open the app, email us from the address you sign in with, so we can confirm it's your account:

Request deletion

We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your sign-in email and your place in the household.
  • If you're the household's last member: the whole household: its lists, items, trips, prices, stores, templates and barcode links.
  • Otherwise the household's data stays for its other members.

Deletion is permanent. To keep a copy, use Home → Back up everything first. If you never signed in, there's nothing on our servers: uninstalling the app removes everything.

اطلب الحذف

بنمسحه خلال 30 يوم وبنرد عليك نأكد.

إيه اللي بيتمسح

  • حسابك: إيميل الدخول ومكانك في البيت.
  • لو إنت آخر عضو في البيت: البيت كله: القوايم والحاجات والمشاوير والأسعار والمحلات والقوالب وربط الباركود.

الحذف نهائي. عشان تحتفظ بنسخة، استخدم البيت ← اعمل نسخة احتياطية الأول. ولو عمرك ما سجلت دخول، مفيش حاجة على السيرفر: مسح التطبيق بيمسح كل حاجة.
